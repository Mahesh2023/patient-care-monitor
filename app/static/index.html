CARE-MM

Privacy-first multimodal patient monitoring for caregivers. Video and audio are analysed on this device; only numeric signals leave the browser.

Two-factor authentication is enabled. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes.

Research / educational use only. Not FDA-approved. Not a medical device.
